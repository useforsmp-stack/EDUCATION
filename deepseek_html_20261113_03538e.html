Methodology & data integrity

Session date will appear after loading.
  • Premium change = (close − previous close) ÷ previous close × 100. Contracts with missing or zero previous close, missing close, or non-STO instruments are excluded.
  • Only NSE UDiFF STO (stock-option) contracts are included. IDO index options and futures are excluded.
  • Strike count is the number of distinct strikes per stock across CE/PE and expiries; strike-expiry pairs are counted separately.
  • Stock ranking: distinct rising strikes, then distinct strike-expiry combinations, then average premium change. Alternative ranking modes are provided.
  • Volume and OI are session totals; summed values over rising contracts should not be interpreted as directional order flow.
  • High-volatility view uses available high/low fields. If OHLC fields are absent, only premium change is used for volatility ranking.
  • Clicking a stock symbol opens a Google News search. This tool is an informational scan, not a trade recommendation.
Official source: NSE All Reports – Derivatives. No stale-session substitution is performed.
